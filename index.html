Install on Android

1
Download the app

Tap Download for Android above on your phone.

2
Open the downloaded file

If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps, then tap Install.

3
Say hi to your mascot

Sign in so your ideas sync with the Chrome extension and your friends.

Had an older test version (1.2)? Uninstall it once before installing 1.4.

Chrome extension

1
Download and unzip

Download extension (.zip)

Double-click the zip so it becomes a folder.

2
Open extensions and turn on Developer mode

Go to chrome://extensions and switch Developer mode on, top right.

3
Load unpacked

Click Load unpacked, choose the unzipped folder, then pin Little Later from the puzzle icon. Sign in with the same account as your phone.

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc.
