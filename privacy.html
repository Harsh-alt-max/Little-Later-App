Privacy Policy for Little Later

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Little Later ("we", "our", or "the app") helps you save links, organize ideas into personal and shared folders, and set reminders to revisit them. We respect your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: if you sign in with Google or email, we store your name and email address to identify your account, back up your ideas and connect shared folders.
  • Your content: the links, notes, to-dos, reminders, folder names, streak and mascot settings you create. They are stored on your device and, if you sign in, in Google Firebase so they sync across your devices and with people you share folders with.
  • Link titles: when you save a link, the app asks that website (or YouTube) for the page title so your idea has a readable name.
  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled on your phone. We do not track your location, contacts or files.

2. How We Use It

  • To save, sync and remind you about your ideas across your phone and the Chrome extension.
  • To let people you invite see and add to a shared folder.
  • We never sell, rent or trade your information, and we show no ads.

3. Storage and Security

Cloud data is hosted on Google Firebase. Your backup can only be read by your signed-in account. A shared folder can be read by signed-in people who have its invite link.

4. Deleting Your Data

Delete any idea or folder in the app at any time. To delete your account and data, open Profile → Delete account, or email us.
