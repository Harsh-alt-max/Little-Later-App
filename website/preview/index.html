Little Later
Try the new design

Everything here works, and your changes stay in this browser. Pick a look, then use it like the real app.

  1. Type or paste in the bar on Today: “call mom 9pm tomorrow” or any link. The time lights up as you type.
  2. Tap the mic above the + and just say it. Lil listens, then adds it. Say a few things at once (“dairy at 7 am and school at 10”) and pick reminders or one to-do list.
  3. Voice speaks Hindi too (tap EN to switch): “kal dopahar 12 baje doodh lana hai”. Tap the words to fix them.
  4. Tap the flame for your streak, hearts, outfits and Lil’s name.
  5. Tap Done on the big card, or the clock for Later (same time tomorrow).
  6. Tap any reminder for its details: Done, Edit, Share, Delete.
  7. Saved filters: Links, To-dos, Images. New folder: pick a colour and an icon.
