Little Later
save it now, enjoy it later.

Save links, reminders and to-dos from any app. Your little mascot brings them back right when you have time.

Things to try

  1. Pull down on Today and hold. Lil peeks over the top and blinks.
  2. Tap + to save a link, a reminder or a to-do. Reminders and to-dos can repeat every day or on the days you pick.
  3. Open Saved and tap the top-right button for list, grid and big-cover views.
  4. Open Folders → Goa trip, a folder shared with friends, with its own chat.
  5. Tap Lil on Today to say hi and keep your streak. Profile has the sounds, quiet hours and themes.

This is the real Android app running in your browser with sample data. Nothing you do here is saved. Phone-only things (notifications, sharing from other apps, vibration) don't work in the browser.
