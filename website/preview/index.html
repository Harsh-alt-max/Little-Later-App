Little Later
Try the app

This is the real Android app, running in your browser with sample things in it. Nothing you do here is saved.

  1. Type or paste in the bar on Today: “call mom 9pm tomorrow” or any link. The time lights up as you type.
  2. Tap the mic and talk, in English or Hindi. It keeps listening through pauses until you tap Add. Chrome will ask to use your microphone.
  3. Say a few things at once (“kal 3 baje doodh lana aur 5 baje gym jana”). Tap any row to fix it, or + to add one by hand.
  4. Tap Done on the big card, or swipe it for Later.
  5. Open a to-do list to tick, change or add steps.
  6. Tap the flame for your streak and Lil’s outfits. Folders have their own colours; “Goa trip” is shared.
