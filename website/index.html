How it works

Don’t fill a form.
Just say it.

Little Later understands how you actually talk, times, days, Hinglish and all. Say three things in one breath and it makes three reminders.

ENEnglish

1

Tap the mic and talk

Pause, think, carry on. Lil keeps listening until you tap Add, so nothing you say gets lost.

2

It finds the times for you

“kal dopahar 12 baje”, “tomorrow 9pm”, “friday 10am”. Times light up as you speak.

3

Fix anything with a tap

Change a word or a time, remove a row, or add one by hand. Keep them as reminders or make one to-do list.

4

It rings when it’s time

A full-screen reminder you can’t miss: Done, 15 minutes, 1 hour or tonight.

Questions

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription, nothing to buy. The hearts and outfits for Lil are earned by using the app, not with money.

Why isn’t it on the Play Store?

Little Later is in early access. We’re checking that people find it useful before it goes to the stores, so for now it comes straight from this page. It’s the same app, signed by us, and updates install over the old version.

Do I need an account?

No. Pick “Continue as guest” and everything stays on your phone. Sign in with Google or email only if you want your things on your phone and computer, or to share folders with friends.

Does it understand Hindi?

Yes, typed or spoken, in Hindi or Hinglish: “kal dopahar 12 baje doodh lana hai”, “shaam ko 4 baje”, “parso subah 9 baje”. Tap EN on the mic to switch to हिंदी.

What does it do with my data?

Your things are yours. Without an account they never leave your phone. With an account they’re kept in your private space so they sync. The microphone is only used while you’re adding something by voice. Read the privacy policy.

How do I get updates?

Download the APK from this page again and install it over the old one. Your reminders, links and Lil’s streak stay where they are.

I found a bug or have an idea

Please tell us! In the app go to Profile → Send feedback, or email harshksingh100@gmail.com. Early access means your ideas really change what gets built next.
